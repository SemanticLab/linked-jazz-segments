The stories they share

Every passage of the oral histories was embedded and clustered; the 460 stories that came out are laid out here by how alike they are. Inside each one sits everybody who told part of that story. Hover a face for what they said, click for all of it.

Loading the map…

The stories are HDBSCAN clusters of at least ten passages over the segment embeddings (interview openings, tape changes and copyright pages left out; groups the naming model judged to have no single subject are marked as loose groupings). A person appears in a story when at least one of their passages is a core member of it; the icon grows with their number of passages there. Portraits come from Linked Jazz and Wikidata; everyone else is their initials.
